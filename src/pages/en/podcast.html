NutraMEA Conversations

Long form interviews with the people who move supplements through this region: regulatory affairs managers, distributors, contract manufacturers and the consultants who file the dossiers. The brief records what changed. The podcast asks the people involved why, and what it cost them.

Format

Length
Forty to sixty minutes, one guest
Language
English and Arabic episodes, each published in the language it was recorded in
Schedule
Two episodes a month
Not included
No sponsored guests. A company appearing on the show has not paid to be there.
