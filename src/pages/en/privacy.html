Privacy

This page describes what this website does today. It is short because the site does very little. Last updated 18 September 2026.

What is collected

Your email
Only if you type it into the subscribe form. It is used to send the weekly brief and nothing else, and it is not sold, rented or shared with advertisers.
Server logs
The host that serves this site records requests, including IP address, in the ordinary way. These logs are used to keep the site running.
Email you send us
Messages to our address are kept so we can answer them and keep a record of corrections.

What is not collected

This site sets no cookies of its own, runs no analytics, and carries no advertising or social tracking pixels. Nothing on it builds a profile of you.

One third party

The typeface is served by Google Fonts, so loading a page makes a request to Google, which sees your IP address. That is the only outside service this site contacts. We plan to serve the typeface ourselves and remove it.

Removing your data

Every brief carries an unsubscribe link, which removes your address. You can also write to us and ask for the address to be deleted, or ask what we hold for it, and we will do it without asking why.
