About NutraMEA Int.

NutraMEA Intelligence is a business to business information service for the nutraceutical trade in the Middle East and North Africa. It exists because the rules that decide whether a supplement can be sold in this region are published in nine places, in two languages, on no shared schedule, and rarely in a form anyone can act on directly.

How it works

  • Read the source

    Authority circulars, decisions, banned ingredient lists and public registers are read on a fixed schedule rather than when something makes the news.

  • Record the change

    Each change becomes one entry with a source document, a publication date, an effective date and the market it applies to. An entry without a source does not get published.

  • Publish twice

    Entries go into the searchable record as they are logged, and into the Tuesday brief as a written summary for people who would rather not search.

What this is not

Not legal advice
Entries describe what an authority published. They do not tell you whether your product complies, and they do not replace a regulatory consultant.
Not a filing service
We do not submit dossiers or represent products in front of an authority.
Not pay to appear
Companies cannot buy an entry, a mention in the brief or a seat on the podcast.
Not complete
Coverage is nine markets and the categories listed under coverage. Anything outside that is not tracked, and we say so rather than implying otherwise.

Getting in touch

Corrections are welcome and are acted on. If an entry is wrong, write to us with the source and it is fixed in the record, with a note in the next issue.
